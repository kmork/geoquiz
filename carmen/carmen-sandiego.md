# 🕵️‍♀️ Carmen Sandiego

## Alias: The Crimson Catastrophe
## 🧾 Background File
*Highly Classified, Mostly Because It’s Embarrassing*

Once upon a time, Carmen Sandiego was not the world’s most elusive master thief…

She was a tour guide.

Yes. A very enthusiastic tour guide.

---

## 🎤 The Origin of Evil™

Carmen worked at a global travel agency called **“WanderWise”**, where she specialized in:

- Over-explaining historical monuments
- Dramatically reenacting ancient events
- Correcting tourists mid-sentence with *“Actually…”*

Her breaking point came when a tourist asked:

> “So… is the Eiffel Tower in Italy?”

Carmen paused.
Smiled.

And something inside her… snapped like a cheap souvenir keychain.

---

## 🌍 The Grand Plan

Carmen decided:

> “If people won’t learn geography… I will REMOVE geography.”

Her master plan:

- Steal the world’s most famous landmarks
- Hide them in secret locations
- Force the world to finally pay attention

Because nothing says *“education”* like:
👉 waking up and realizing the Colosseum is missing

---

## 🧥 Signature Style

Carmen’s iconic red trench coat isn’t just fashion—it’s tactical:

- 37 hidden pockets
- 12 disguises
- 1 emergency croissant compartment

Her hat?
Aerodynamically useless, but dramatically essential.

---

## 🚗 Getaway Method

Carmen never escapes the same way twice, but her favorites include:

- Red convertible *(for flair)*
- High-speed gondola *(don’t ask)*
- “Borrowed” museum exhibits

---

## 🎯 Weakness

Despite her genius, Carmen has one fatal flaw:

> She cannot resist correcting people.

**Agent:** “I think we’re in Spain—”
**Carmen (from the shadows):**
👉 “Actually, this region was part of Aragon in the 15th century.”

…and just like that, she reveals herself.
